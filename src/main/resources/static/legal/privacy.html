Privacy Policy — DoneBot

Last updated: 2026-04-30

This Privacy Policy describes how DoneBot ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the DoneBot mobile application (the "App").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, profile avatar, authentication tokens.
  • Tasks & groups: titles, descriptions, dates, recurrence settings, completion status, group memberships, and roles you assign.
  • Chat with DoneBot (AI assistant): messages you send to the in-app DoneBot assistant are sent to Google’s Gemini API for processing. Conversation history is stored locally on your device and on our backend for chat continuity.
  • Device & push tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens, device identifiers, and app version.
  • Diagnostic data: Firebase Crashlytics crash reports and Firebase Analytics usage events. You can opt out from app settings (when available).

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your data in sync across devices.
  • To deliver task reminders, group invitations, and ownership change notifications.
  • To process AI chat requests via Google Gemini (input is sent to Google for inference, but is not used to train Google’s models per their Vertex AI terms).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability.

3. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Group members: tasks, comments, and activity within a group are visible to all members of that group.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (auth, FCM, Crashlytics, Analytics, AI Logic), Render (backend hosting), and standard infrastructure subprocessors.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account. When you do:

  • All your personal tasks, chat messages, group memberships, and device tokens are deleted within the same operation.
  • Groups you own are either transferred to another member (admin role passes randomly to a remaining member, with admins preferred) or deleted entirely if you were the only member.
  • Refresh tokens and active sessions are revoked.
  • Backups may retain residual data for up to 30 days for disaster recovery.

5. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. For deletion, the in-app flow is the fastest path. For other requests, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

DoneBot is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted in-app where feasible.

8. Contact

Questions? Reach us at support@donebot.app.
